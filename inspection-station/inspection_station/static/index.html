      Accounts

      One account per person, so the audit trail shows who did what. Operators log in with a 4-6 digit PIN and can run the line, choose recipes and start batches. Engineers log in with a password and can change everything. Accounts are turned off, never deleted, so old records keep their name.

      Operators are logged out after 30 minutes without activity, engineers after 15. Five wrong attempts lock an account for 5 minutes.

      Changes apply immediately, are saved on the station and recorded in the audit trail.
