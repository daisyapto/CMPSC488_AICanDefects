Inspection Line Simulator

A working model of the proposed station for both solutions in the requirements specification: Solution 1 (KEYENCE VS-C320MX, CV-L3, CA-DRM5X) and Solution 2, the Teledyne budget option (Genie Nano C1280, Arducam C-mount lens, Python/OpenCV on a Windows PC). The single-file conveyor runs at its normal speed with no added gaps, every can is checked against the 14 defect types in the Defect Requirements, and the result is carried to the Yards air-jet rejector by a calibrated delay. Switch tabs to watch the same run from the line, through the camera, in 3D, on a timing trace, or as production statistics.

    Tracking and faults

    The conveyor runs at its normal production speed with no added gaps, as stated in the requirements; the speed offset slider is only for testing what happens if the speed is not constant. Lighting effects are illustrative: LumiTrax lowers noise on shape defects (dents, bends, cracks, indentations), Multi-Spectrum lowers noise on contrast defects (stains, debris, scratches), and Normal lighting has the most glare. Replace these factors with the lighting comparison from Part 2 §2.4 once it is measured.

    Virtual test bench

    Each test runs the simulation headless with a fixed random seed, so results repeat exactly. Negative controls switch a safeguard off, or leave a known gap open, and pass when the expected failure appears. “Missed (visible)” counts defects a camera could see but the system passed; defects outside every camera's view are reported separately as a coverage gap.

    Model assumptions: 220 cans/min at 248.9 mm/s (49 ft/min) gives a 67.9 mm can pitch, a 1.9 mm gap between 66.0 mm cans. Image scale uses a pinhole model: KEYENCE VS-C320MX with CV-L3 (3.5 mm, 1/1.8 in, 3.45 µm, 2048 × 1536, 62 fps) gives about 0.099 mm per pixel at 100 mm; Teledyne Genie Nano C1280 with an Arducam C-mount lens (1/2 in, 4.8 µm, 1280 × 1024, 92 fps) gives about 0.096 mm per pixel with an 8 mm lens at 160 mm. The Teledyne option assumes 1.3× edge-measurement noise (colour sensor) and 70 ± 20 ms processing on the PC; sensor response times are from the datasheets (E3Z-D61 1 ms, LR-ZB250 1.5–50 ms, E18-D80NK under 2 ms). The trigger switches on when a can's centre is 30 mm upstream of the beam. Anomaly scores model the example ranges in the Defect Requirements. Valve delay, push success, processing time and lighting factors are placeholders. None of these values are Keyence outputs.
